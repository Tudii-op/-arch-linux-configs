# My ML4W Hyprland dotfiles

Personal setup on top of [ML4W dotfiles](https://github.com/mylinuxforwork/dotfiles) (stable, Hyprland Lua config).

## What's customized
- **Themes:** Sakura and Crimson (Hypr borders, waybar, swaync, wallpaper)
- **Hyprland:** gradient borders, rounding 10, blur/shadow, opacity 0.95/0.75, custom keybinds; `hypr/custom.lua` for personal additions
- **Quickshell status bar:** autohide, custom module layout, edited stats/updates/logo modules; dock disabled
- **Cava music visualizer on the wallpaper:** glowing bars along the bottom of the screen (`extras/bin/cava-wallpaper`, `glow_bars.frag`, peak caps), drawn by the custom `cava-gl`; `BACKEND=terminal` in the launcher brings back the old kitty-panel bars. Toggle with `SUPER+CTRL+V`
- **Cava circle visualizer:** `extras/bin/cava-circle` runs a spinning ring (`prism_ring.frag`: mirrored spikes, wavy contour rings, bass-pulsing core) plus mirrored "wings" of bars on both sides at its height (`mirror_wings.frag`), on transparent layer-shell surfaces; toggles together with the bars on `SUPER+CTRL+V`. Needs the custom `cava-gl` binary: run `extras/bin/build-cava-gl` (builds [Tudii-op/cava@layer-shell-glsl](https://github.com/Tudii-op/cava/tree/layer-shell-glsl) into `~/.local/bin/cava-gl`)
- **Visualizer colors from the wallpaper:** `extras/bin/cava-colors` picks colors from the wallpaper pixels behind each visualizer after every wallpaper change (matugen post_hooks); `cava-gl` makes them flow faster with louder music and brighten on hits. Needs ImageMagick (`magick`)
- **matugen:** extra templates (cava, oh-my-posh, quickshell overview, ML4W settings)
- **extras/**: Neovim config, cava configs + shaders, and the `cava-wallpaper` / `cava-circle` / `cava-colors` / `build-cava-gl` scripts (live outside the ML4W dir; copy to `~/.config/nvim`, `~/.config/cava`, `~/.local/bin`)

Colors files are generated by matugen and are git-ignored; stock wallpapers are ignored except my own.
